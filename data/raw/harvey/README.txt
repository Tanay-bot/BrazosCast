Flood Map Files for Brazos River for the August and September 2017 flood event in Texas
   
Contains shapefiles and raster file
U.S. Geological Survey Data Release https://doi.org/10.5066/F7VH5N3N
       
Geographic Information files associated with USGS report:
Watson, K.M., Harwell, G.R., Wallace, D.S., Welborn, T.L., Stengel, V.G., and McDowell, J.S., 2018, Characterization
of peak streamflows and flood inundation of selected areas in southeastern Texas and southwestern Louisiana
from the August and September 2017 flood resulting from Hurricane Harvey: U.S. Geological Survey Scientific
Investigations Report 2018–5070, 44 p., https://doi.org/10.3133/sir20185070. 


The mapped area boundary, flood inundation extents, and depth rasters were created to provide an estimated extent of flood inundation along the Brazos River within the communities in Austin, Fort Bend, Waller, 
and Brazoria Counties, Texas. 

These geospatial data include the following items: 
1. bnd_brazos; shapefile containing the polygon showing the mapped area boundary for the Brazos River flood maps (one for upper and lower), 
2. hwm_brazos; shapefile containing high-water mark points (one for the Brazos River), 
3. polygon_brazos; shapefile containing mapped extent of flood inundation, derived from the water-surface elevation surveyed at high-water marks (one for upper and lower), and 
4. brazos_depth; raster file for the flood depths derived from the water-surface elevation surveyed at high-water marks (one for upper and lower).

The upstream and downstream mapped area extent is limited to the upstream-most and downstream-most high-water mark locations. In areas of uncertainty of flood extent, the mapped area boundary is lined up with 
the flood inundation polygon extent. The mapped area boundary polygon was used to extract the final flood inundation polygon and depth raster from the water-surface elevation raster file. Depth raster files were 
created using the "Topo to Raster" tool in ArcMap (ESRI, 2012).

Flood documentation maps were created using GIS to show the extent of inundation along impacted river reaches in Texas following the August and September, 2017 Hurricane Harvey flood event. 
Flood-inundation maps are intended to estimate the areal extent and depth of flooding that correspond to the HWMs identified and surveyed by USGS hydrographers following the flood event. 
The mapped area boundary, flood inundation extents, and depth rasters were created to support the development of flood inundation maps for the Federal Emergency Management Agency's (FEMA) 
response and recovery operations following the August and September, 2017 flood event in Texas.
